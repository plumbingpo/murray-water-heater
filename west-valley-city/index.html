Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in West Valley City

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually encodes the month and year it was built, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units about 20. If your heater is past that point and also shows discolored water, popping sounds, or dampness under the tank, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install once the tank fails.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Hot Water Headaches West Valley City Homeowners Face

Whatever type of water heater your West Valley City home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
