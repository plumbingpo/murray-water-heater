Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water
